# Probabilistic Ritual

A mathematical formula granting limited foreknowledge of a future event. Known by [ROBERT](/Birdcage_Casefiles/PCs/Bo_Bextiyar.html).

## Activation

**Cost:** 6 WP, 1D4 SAN

Roll a D100 and record the value.

## Effects

For INT hours after activation, you may replace the result of a single D100 roll with the recorded number — before or after rolling. Doing so incurs a further 0/D4 SAN loss from **Helplessness**, as the deterministic nature of the universe is made painfully clear to you.
