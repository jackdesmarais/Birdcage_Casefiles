# The Song of Tuonetar

An elaborate ritual chant in proto-Uralic, addressed to Tuonetar, Finnish goddess of death and the underworld. Learned from the [Hymnal of Tuonetar](/Birdcage_Casefiles/items/Hymnal_of_Tuonetar.html), acquired from a [Kuoleman Enkelit](/Birdcage_Casefiles/factions/Kuoleman_Enkelit.html) shaman's computer during [Operation Minimalism](/Birdcage_Casefiles/session_notes/DG_Casefiles/2026_05_31_Minimalism1.html).

## Study

**Complexity:** Elaborate

**Time Required:** Weeks

**Skills Gained:** Unnatural +1%

**SAN Cost:** 1D10

## Activation

**Time Required:** 1 minute

**Cost:** 9 WP, 1D4 SAN/day chant continues

## Effects

No one within earshot of the chant when it begins can die while the chant continues.

- Any time the chanter takes non-self-inflicted damage while the effect holds, roll SAN. On a failure, take no physical damage but take an equivalent amount as SAN damage.
- If the chanter's HP is reduced to zero while under this effect, they immediately rise as a **wight**: STR and CON raise to 24, HP instantly restores to full. Roll POW:
  - **Failure:** Begin rotting. INT and CHA reduce by 1 per day.
  - **Success:** Stable. No further rot.
  - In either case: die instantly if the chant stops, no longer heal naturally, and cannot rise again.
- All temporary breaks while under this effect produce **fight** responses.
