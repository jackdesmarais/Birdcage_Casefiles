You are high as a fucking kite off of amphetamines and flying down the highway at 125 miles per hour. There's a severed head in the trunk and it's screaming and chanting in proto-Uralic and you tried killing yourself an hour ago and three bullets to the head later you are feeling god damn fine.

# Session Notes

We began with a series of flashbacks until we arrived at the present moment (July 10 2005).

### Activations

[EISENHOWER](/Birdcage_Casefiles/PCs/Raymund_Ushas.html) was just back from an overseas deployment, he was at the range blowing off steam when he found an envelope at the bottom a box of shells inviting him to a night at the opera in Albuquerque. On the way home he received an email assigning him to a training exercise in New Mexico and telling him to report to a C130 for transport.

[ROBERT](/Birdcage_Casefiles/PCs/Bo_Bextiyar.html) was playing JENGA with his coworkers when he crit failed his dexterity and sent the tower flying. He looked down to see a green triangle sharpied onto the block he had pulled, his activation signal. At that moment his boss told him that there had been a failure in an air-gapped system they installed on a New Mexico base. Since the system is air-gapped and he is on call this weekend he has to get on a flight to fix it in person.

### The Setup

[EISENHOWER](/Birdcage_Casefiles/PCs/Raymund_Ushas.html), [ROBERT](/Birdcage_Casefiles/PCs/Bo_Bextiyar.html), [SHALIMAR](/Birdcage_Casefiles/PCs/Patricia_Gomez.html), and [EREBUS](/Birdcage_Casefiles/PCs/Barry_Vredenberg.html) report to a conference room where they are informed that reports have been filtering in of an [eastern european biker gang](/Birdcage_Casefiles/factions/Kuoleman_Enkelit.html) operating just outside Albuquerque who have been getting in altercations with law enforcement. Normally this wouldn't be their remit, however reports have begun to mention officers responding with lethal force to no effect. Their mission is to assess the presence of unnatural influence, contain any influence they discover, cover it up, and if possible within those dictates preserve civillian lives.

Since [SHALIMAR](/Birdcage_Casefiles/PCs/Patricia_Gomez.html) was in progress setting up a sting operation on the bikers, they decided to use this as a cover to infiltrate and take them out. But first they needed to confirm unnaturalness. [ROBERT](/Birdcage_Casefiles/PCs/Bo_Bextiyar.html) hacked the cellphone network to id a meth deal going down and they went in with a SWAT team provoking a shootout. They saw a biker fall back chanting while the others took bullets without falling. They then met with the group posing as representatives of a group trying to move meth and guns with the bikers. They were told that to prove they weren't undercover, they needed to go with the bikers on a job to rob a truck.

They see a 16 wheeler being escorted by two escalades, [EISENHOWER](/Birdcage_Casefiles/PCs/Raymund_Ushas.html) now up on some meth quickly ids this as an airforce convoy. They go in hot. One of the bikers begins chanting and a massive fire fight breaks out. In the battle, [EREBUS](/Birdcage_Casefiles/PCs/Barry_Vredenberg.html) blows up the chanter and grabs the head, but catches a bullet in the gut. [EISENHOWER](/Birdcage_Casefiles/PCs/Raymund_Ushas.html) gets carried away and kills some of the airforce guys and breaks down attempting suicide and gaining massive strength and con. Then the truck goes off road and splits open. Out of it rises something like a [crab made of fungus](/Birdcage_Casefiles/NPCs/FUNGUS_CRAB.html) that stop motions itself into the sky firing lightning bolts at the bikers. In the fighting something not unlike a lobster gets blown off the fungus crab and [SHALIMAR](/Birdcage_Casefiles/PCs/Patricia_Gomez.html) grabs it. The crew piles into an escalade and takes off. The head still in the back chanting.

## The Op

[ROBERT](/Birdcage_Casefiles/PCs/Bo_Bextiyar.html) manages to ID the chanting as some sort of relative of Finnish and Hungarian, and manages to remote into the shaman's computer to rip a copy of an epub that appears to be written in the same language. He then begins trying to bootstrap his way to a translation.

The group realizes that 4 of the bikers are on their tail and [EISENHOWER](/Birdcage_Casefiles/PCs/Raymund_Ushas.html) pops out the driver's side window to start exchanging fire with the bikers. He hits them but doesn't manage to take any down. The one he hits goes berserk jumps on the car trying to punch his way through the window. [EREBUS](/Birdcage_Casefiles/PCs/Barry_Vredenberg.html) dives into the drivers side to try to stop the car from going off the road but the car slows allowing a second biker to jump on. [ROBERT](/Birdcage_Casefiles/PCs/Bo_Bextiyar.html) decodes enough of the text to determine that it is hymns to Tuonetar. [ROBERT](/Birdcage_Casefiles/PCs/Bo_Bextiyar.html) tried to throw a tire iron at the attacking bikers but bounced it off the inside of the car bruising himself and [SHALIMAR](/Birdcage_Casefiles/PCs/Patricia_Gomez.html). [SHALIMAR](/Birdcage_Casefiles/PCs/Patricia_Gomez.html) blasts away the two remaining bikers with lightning bolts from the lobster but then runs out of teeth. [EISENHOWER](/Birdcage_Casefiles/PCs/Raymund_Ushas.html) takes a hit and goes berserk as well climbing onto the roof of the car to engage the bikers and pulling the pin on a frag grenade before tackling them off the car. The fall and blast annihilate him and one of the bikers but the other gets up and begins sprinting after the car in a rage until he disappears over the horizon.

They then arrive at the [Albuquerque Green Box](/Birdcage_Casefiles/locations/Albuquerque_Green_Box.html), a bunker made of buried shipping containers accessed by a padlocked horizontal house door. [EREBUS](/Birdcage_Casefiles/PCs/Barry_Vredenberg.html) smashes the door open with a fire axe and they descend inside. They try to quickly search the box and find a variety of items but take too long searching and begin to hear helicopters overhead. [EREBUS](/Birdcage_Casefiles/PCs/Barry_Vredenberg.html) destroys the chanting head (cut scene to bullet riddled bikers suddenly falling, and [EISENHOWER's](/Birdcage_Casefiles/PCs/Raymund_Ushas.html) hand stopping its slow crawl to the side of the road). [ROBERT](/Birdcage_Casefiles/PCs/Bo_Bextiyar.html) begins chanting himself. The group climbs the ladder seeing the [FUNGUS CRAB](/Birdcage_Casefiles/NPCs/FUNGUS_CRAB.html) and a black hawk disembarking a squad of USAF operatives appearing to coordinate with it. [SHALIMAR](/Birdcage_Casefiles/PCs/Patricia_Gomez.html) beat the opposing forces in a dex check to act first and snapped a finger they found in the [Albuquerque Green Box](/Birdcage_Casefiles/locations/Albuquerque_Green_Box.html) setting off a blast that nuked the USAF forces and fungus crab into radioactive dust.
