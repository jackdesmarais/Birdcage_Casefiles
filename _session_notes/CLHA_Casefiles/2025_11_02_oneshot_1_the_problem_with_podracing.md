# Mission Brief

You are in a back room of a small community center, the floors are alternating squares of cream and eggshell linoleum that manage to look dirty even when just cleaned and overhead is a field of drop ceiling tiles broken up by ambiguous brown stains and flickering fluorescents. A rough semi-circle of folding chairs sits in front of a rolling whiteboard.

The squeak of tennis shoes on linoleum alerts you to the approach of a woman of average height. As she reaches the white board she falls into an at ease position so sharply you could swear for a second she was wearing fatigues instead of a cardigan, jeans, and sketchers.

"Alright, thank you all for attending this off schedule meeting of the [City Living History Association's](/Birdcage_Casefiles/factions/City_Living_History_Association.html) emerging opportunities committee. As you may be aware we have been receiving 'complaints' from 'event attendees' indicating that some of our 'exhibits' are not 'age-appropriate'. As such we have been looking for opportunities to 'expand our target audience'.

With that, I have today's emerging opportunity.
In [REDACTED], [Maddie Holmes](/Birdcage_Casefiles/NPCs/Maddie_Holmes.html) gained control of [The Damn Vannes](/Birdcage_Casefiles/factions/The_Damn_Vannes.html) after the death of [Sean Claude](/Birdcage_Casefiles/NPCs/Sean_Claude.html) due to a sabotaged brake line while competing in the Chelsea Classic Pod Race. We have focus group results suggesting that our 'exhibits' would be 'appropriate for a broader audience' if instead of a fatal crash due to a cut brake line, [Sean Claude](/Birdcage_Casefiles/NPCs/Sean_Claude.html) had a fatal crash due to reckless acceleration around a turn in the course. It is of vital importance that [Maddie Holmes](/Birdcage_Casefiles/NPCs/Maddie_Holmes.html) and her co-conspirators [Pierce Sisnan](/Birdcage_Casefiles/NPCs/Pierce_Sisnan.html) and [Buck Morris](/Birdcage_Casefiles/NPCs/Buck_Morris.html) do not detect the interference until it is too late or they may try to intervene.

As always if you are captured we will disavow any association with your actions, and you are authorized to use any and all force to accomplish your mission, but we strongly recommend that you avoid overt action as replacing agents lost due to paradox is a strain on [CLHA](/Birdcage_Casefiles/factions/City_Living_History_Association.html) resources. Finally, keep your eyes out for other agents — we will as usual be offering extra drink tickets at the upcoming [CLHA](/Birdcage_Casefiles/factions/City_Living_History_Association.html) picnic to anyone who can positively identify the agents and targets of either the Official Fan Club of the Seattle Mariners or the Dairy Lobby."

# Summary

We did a quick tour of FATE and made characters (quick mode with just a high concept, trouble and max skill). We then dove in! The crew went back in time, [Cheboigan](/Birdcage_Casefiles/PCs/Cheboigan.html) got on the Jumbotron, [IForgot](/Birdcage_Casefiles/PCs/IForgot.html) indulged in his rampant alcoholism, and [Gecko](/Birdcage_Casefiles/PCs/Gecko.html) evaded [Brock](/Birdcage_Casefiles/PCs/Brock.html) to steal a hooligan's wallet so he could stake $50 and fully punched Nathan's hotdog loyalty card on a prop bet that someone would jump the east river during the Chelsea Classic Pod Race. They then managed to sneak into the backstage of the Chelsea Pod Racing stadium, on the way noticing someone with a Seattle Mariners pin (a possible enemy agent?). [Gecko](/Birdcage_Casefiles/PCs/Gecko.html) and [IForgot](/Birdcage_Casefiles/PCs/IForgot.html) managed to bluff their way under [Sean Claude's](/Birdcage_Casefiles/NPCs/Sean_Claude.html) F650 by saying they were part of the race performance enhancing mods check team. [Cheboigan](/Birdcage_Casefiles/PCs/Cheboigan.html) convinced [Buck Morris](/Birdcage_Casefiles/NPCs/Buck_Morris.html) that they were also members of the Ashorian resistance while [Brock](/Birdcage_Casefiles/PCs/Brock.html) distracted [Maddie Holmes](/Birdcage_Casefiles/NPCs/Maddie_Holmes.html) and [Pierce Sisnan](/Birdcage_Casefiles/NPCs/Pierce_Sisnan.html) so that [IForgot](/Birdcage_Casefiles/PCs/IForgot.html) could fix the sabotaged brake line and wire the turbocharger and NOS injectors into the power steering. They then headed out to try to identify opposition agents for extra drink tickets! [Brock](/Birdcage_Casefiles/PCs/Brock.html) identified Dairy Lobby agents replacing all the low fat and non-dairy milk options in concessions with whole milk. [Cheboigan](/Birdcage_Casefiles/PCs/Cheboigan.html) discovered agents of the Official Fan Club of the Seattle Mariners searching the audience for the Original Rally Shoe. [IForgot](/Birdcage_Casefiles/PCs/IForgot.html) found dairy agents sabotaging the Planet Oat sponsored Pod. [Gecko](/Birdcage_Casefiles/PCs/Gecko.html) went up to the Fan Duel VIP lounge to look for agents but got distracted when [Sean Claude](/Birdcage_Casefiles/NPCs/Sean_Claude.html) lost control of his pod coming up to the Williamsburg bridge and jumped the river before eventually losing control in Long Island City and exploding in a fireball against a large residential building. While this means [Gecko](/Birdcage_Casefiles/PCs/Gecko.html) won't get any extra drink tickets, it also means his prop bet pays off — he earns $5k and 1,000 hotdogs!
