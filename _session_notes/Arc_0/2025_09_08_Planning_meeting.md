# Session Notes

We discussed structures for the campaign and decided a good structure would be to run one-shots where teams of time travelers jump back into the timeline on missions. We then use *Microscope: Echo* to ripple the effects of their visit through the timeline. This gives a good structure to the campaign with lots of episodic style play that doesn't require ongoing plot while keeping a continuity going.

We are keeping it open as to how many time travel organizations there are and what their goals are (it can vary one-shot to one-shot).

As for the time travel model, we will work with a one-true-timeline model, so that there is no tracking of multiple timelines. Paradoxes will be handled on a one-shot by one-shot basis, but the basic model is that you can change the past and these changes ripple forward — in some cases leaving the time traveler as an anomaly, in other cases triggering looper-style fuzzy correction, and in some cases it will turn out this was always the way it was supposed to happen.
