# Session Notes

We ran a session of *Microscope* to build out the history of the city!

We established a history in which a mysterious character [The Milkman](/Birdcage_Casefiles/NPCs/The_Milkman.html) is sighted as [Sean Claude's](/Birdcage_Casefiles/NPCs/Sean_Claude.html) paramilitary organization [The Damn Vannes](/Birdcage_Casefiles/factions/The_Damn_Vannes.html) rise to power in the city. [Sean Claude](/Birdcage_Casefiles/NPCs/Sean_Claude.html) is assassinated by a cabal of his closest allies ([Maddie Holmes](/Birdcage_Casefiles/NPCs/Maddie_Holmes.html), [Buck Morris](/Birdcage_Casefiles/NPCs/Buck_Morris.html), and [Pierce Sisnan](/Birdcage_Casefiles/NPCs/Pierce_Sisnan.html)). [Maddie Holmes](/Birdcage_Casefiles/NPCs/Maddie_Holmes.html) takes over the group and uses a super magnet to obliterate the small town where [Pierce Sisnan](/Birdcage_Casefiles/NPCs/Pierce_Sisnan.html) turned her and her lover in to the authorities.

[The Milkman](/Birdcage_Casefiles/NPCs/The_Milkman.html) departs, revealing that his goal this entire time was to make sure [Pierce Sisnan](/Birdcage_Casefiles/NPCs/Pierce_Sisnan.html) and [Maddie Holmes](/Birdcage_Casefiles/NPCs/Maddie_Holmes.html) found each other.
