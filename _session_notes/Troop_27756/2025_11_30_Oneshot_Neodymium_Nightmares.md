# Mission Description

As your parents drive off, you wander into the fluorescent-lit gymnasium of the local elementary school to join the crowd of 14-18 year old hooligans wearing brown vests studded with iron-on patches. As you assemble, a grizzled skinny older man with white five-o'-clock shadow walks out in front of the crowd. It is Dr. Envelop, your troop leader. He declares:

"Hi all, the Troop 27756 leadership council has decided that instead of Dodge Shuriken or Capture the Katana today we will be working on the Covert Historical Action merit badge. Our historical change will be starting a war between [The Damn Vannes](/Birdcage_Casefiles/factions/The_Damn_Vannes.html) and a rival paramilitary group. We will do this by leading a false flag raid on the Mountain Pass rare earth metal mine producing cerium, lanthanum, neodymium, and europium where [The Damn Vannes](/Birdcage_Casefiles/factions/The_Damn_Vannes.html) have a controlling interest."

# Notes

The troop traveled back to the Mountain Pass mine in the Mojave desert. [Shortfanny](/Birdcage_Casefiles/PCs/Shortfanny.html) headed to the diner to add powerful laxatives from his personal supply to the dinner's sloppy joe mix while [Russel](/Birdcage_Casefiles/PCs/Russel.html) went to say hi to the guards at the gate and [Jake The Snake](/Birdcage_Casefiles/PCs/Jake_The_Snake.html) tried to sneak into the mine power plant to plant explosives. [Russel](/Birdcage_Casefiles/PCs/Russel.html) then sprang a surprise attack on the gate guards drawing [Pierce Sisnan's](/Birdcage_Casefiles/NPCs/Pierce_Sisnan.html) desert operatives while [Shortfanny](/Birdcage_Casefiles/PCs/Shortfanny.html) unveiled a powerful magnet and engaged [Sean Claude](/Birdcage_Casefiles/NPCs/Sean_Claude.html) and his Motorcycle Ninja Cavalry. [Jake The Snake](/Birdcage_Casefiles/PCs/Jake_The_Snake.html) sneaked into the power plant and emplaced explosives on the natural gas tanks and steam conduits. As the explosives went off they got beamed up and suffered paradox — they all shrugged it off except [Shortfanny](/Birdcage_Casefiles/PCs/Shortfanny.html), whose nose melted like taffy to dangle by his nipples.
