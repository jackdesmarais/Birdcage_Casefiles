# Hymnal of Tuonetar

A book of hymns to Tuonetar, the Finnish goddess of death and the underworld. Written in proto-Uralic. At least one of the hymns is functional. Acquired by [ROBERT](/Birdcage_Casefiles/PCs/Bo_Bextiyar.html) during [Operation Minimalism](/Birdcage_Casefiles/session_notes/DG_Casefiles/2026_05_31_Minimalism1.html) by remotely copying an epub from a [Kuoleman Enkelit](/Birdcage_Casefiles/factions/Kuoleman_Enkelit.html) shaman's computer.

## Study

- Unnatural +5%

**SAN Cost:** 1D10
