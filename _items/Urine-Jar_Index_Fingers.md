# Urine-Jar Index Fingers

Four severed index fingers suspended in urine inside a mason jar, labeled **"EMERGENCY USE ONLY"**.

Found in the [Albuquerque Green Box](/Birdcage_Casefiles/locations/Albuquerque_Green_Box.html).

## Use

Snapping a finger in half reduces all matter within 15 meters (dome) to radioactive ash, barring a three-meter exclusion zone at the point of detonation.

## Status

**Originally:** 4 fingers

**Expended:** 1, used during [Operation Minimalism](/Birdcage_Casefiles/session_notes/DG_Casefiles/2026_05_31_Minimalism1.html) to destroy [FUNGUS CRAB](/Birdcage_Casefiles/NPCs/FUNGUS_CRAB.html) and [Majestic-12](/Birdcage_Casefiles/factions/Majestic-12.html) forces.

**Remaining:** 3
