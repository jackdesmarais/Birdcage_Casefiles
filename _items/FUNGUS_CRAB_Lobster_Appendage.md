# FUNGUS CRAB Lobster appendage

An appendage resembling a live lobster, blown free from [FUNGUS CRAB](/Birdcage_Casefiles/NPCs/FUNGUS_CRAB.html) during combat outside Albuquerque, New Mexico, July 2005. Recovered from the scene by [SHALIMAR](/Birdcage_Casefiles/PCs/Patricia_Gomez.html).

## Use

Fires lightning bolts. Teeth loaded into the claw serve as ammunition.

Operate with Occult, lethality 25%. requires 1 human fresh human tooth per day or it sickens and dies. Additional 1 per shot.

## History

Used by [SHALIMAR](/Birdcage_Casefiles/PCs/Patricia_Gomez.html) during the pursuit following [Operation Minimalism](/Birdcage_Casefiles/session_notes/DG_Casefiles/2026_05_31_Minimalism1.html) to eliminate two [Kuoleman Enkelit](/Birdcage_Casefiles/factions/Kuoleman_Enkelit.html) bikers who had mounted the team's vehicle.

## Status

**Holder:** [SHALIMAR](/Birdcage_Casefiles/PCs/Patricia_Gomez.html)

**Ammunition:** Expended. Ran out of teeth during the pursuit.
