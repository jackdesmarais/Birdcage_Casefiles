## Special Ability — Probabilistic Ritual

You know a mathematical formula which grants you limited knowledge of a future event. On a successful ritual activation, spend 6 WP and D4 SAN, then roll a D100 and record the value. For INT hours after activating the ritual, you may replace the result of a single D100 roll with the recorded number — before or after rolling. Doing so incurs a further 0/D4 SAN loss from Helplessness, as the deterministic nature of the universe is made painfully clear to you.

## Background

In your day job, you're a computer scientist doing data analysis work for RAND Corporation, America's premier policy think tank. You went digging a bit too deep into some of the corporation's history around the Vietnam War, and attracted the attention of some very scary people. Fortunately, those people decided to offer you a much more interesting job.

Your talents are in systems intrusion. If it's in the air or online, you can probably get it without leaving a trace. Additionally, you're no slouch when it comes to surveillance — bugging phones, planting wiretaps, you've got all the gadgets you need. And if you don't, forget buying more: you can build your own.
