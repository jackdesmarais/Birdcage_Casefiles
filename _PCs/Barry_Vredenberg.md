## Background

In your day job, you are a firefighter. Your strength, speed, resolve, and willingness to keep your mouth shut when instructed to made you a natural recruit to Delta Green. You can save people from dangerous situations, create dangerous situations, and deflect suspicion from the crime scenes you and your fellow Agents create. Nobody suspects that the fireman who called in the bomb threat is the guy that set it. You can make pipe bombs — handmade grenades. None of the components are illegal, but if anyone recognizes the pattern or catches you making them, you're in trouble. And don't get caught carrying them.
