## Background

In your day job, you're an FBI Agent. You spent almost half a year undercover infiltrating a ring of human traffickers and terrorists in San Francisco. The next thing you remember is waking up on a morgue slab after the SFPD Marine Unit pulled your "corpse" out of the Bay. The Bureau weren't the only ones who wanted to know where you'd been.

Your talents are in undercover work and infiltration. On your own, or with the Agency's help, you can set up cover identities and false backstories. You can also sneak around to observe conversations or plant bugs. When all else fails, you're a superb liar.

*In your dreams, you float through a city on the sun, its proud towers streaming with coruscant banners of holy vegetation that flutter and snap in crystal currents.*
