## Background

In your day job, you're a Green Beret — you meet interesting people in other countries and convince them to help you kill people and break things. Delta Green took an interest in you after you killed and broke something that would have killed or broken an ordinary man.

Your chief role in an investigation is to put holes in things which threaten your safety and that of your fellow Agents. You're also decently knowledgeable about the history, customs, and mythology of other cultures. You'll need some kind of excuse to take your military weapons with you when you leave the base, which Delta Green or one of your fellow Agents will hopefully provide. Even without your gear, you're still a very dangerous man.
