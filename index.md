# Birdcage Casefiles
A living campaign wiki for our games set in The Birdcage — a city in our dystopian future where the clouds are swirling metal shards, the rain is rusty shrapnel, and time travel abounds.

## The Setting
The Birdcage is a city with a troubled history: paramilitary factions, political assassinations, rare-earth mineral empires, pod races, and a dairy lobby with labyrinthine ambitions. Multiple organizations send agents into its past to nudge, correct, or exploit its timeline — often working at cross-purposes.

## The Organizations

**[City Living History Association](/Birdcage_Casefiles/factions/City_Living_History_Association.html)** — A Living History Association that puts on exhibits about the history of the city. In order to inncrease "attendence" at their "events" they send agents into the past to "improve audience compatibility".

**[Troop 27756](/Birdcage_Casefiles/factions/Troop_27756.html)** — A boy scout troop that runs Covert Historical Action merit badge missions, sending teenage agents back in time to alter the record.

**Delta Green** — A clandestine US government program whose operators confront the unnatural. Active in the Birdcage's distant past (2005).

**[The Damn Vannes](/Birdcage_Casefiles/factions/The_Damn_Vannes.html)** — The paramilitary organization whose rise and internal power struggle defines much of the Birdcage's recent history.
