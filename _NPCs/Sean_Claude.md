# Sean Claude

Leader of [The Damn Vannes](/Birdcage_Casefiles/factions/The_Damn_Vannes.html), a pod-racing enthusiast, and a man of questionable judgment.

## Aspects
- **Himbo junta honcho**
- **Podracing enthusiast**
- **Prone to half-assery**

## The F650 (3 Refresh)
His custom pod racer.

- **Power train:** +2 to trading paint attacks when directly ramming an opponent
- **Turbo charger:** +1 to Acceleration
- **Truck nuts:** Once per race, create the aspect "I always win at chicken" with one free invoke

## Status
Assassinated by [Maddie Holmes](/Birdcage_Casefiles/NPCs/Maddie_Holmes.html), [Pierce Sisnan](/Birdcage_Casefiles/NPCs/Pierce_Sisnan.html), and [Buck Morris](/Birdcage_Casefiles/NPCs/Buck_Morris.html) via a sabotaged brake line. CLHA agents ensured his death instead resulted from reckless acceleration during [The Problem with Podracing](/Birdcage_Casefiles/session_notes/CLHA_Casefiles/2025_11_02_oneshot_1_the_problem_with_podracing.html).
