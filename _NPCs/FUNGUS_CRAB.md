# FUNGUS CRAB

A massive creature resembling a crab constructed of fungal matter. Escaped from a military convoy truck by a U.S. Air Force unit during an attack {details here} while being transported to Albuquerque, New Mexico, in July 2005.

## Observed Properties

- Stop-motion style locomotion
- Capable of sustained flight
- Fires lightning bolts
- Exhibited high resistance to conventional weapons fire
- One appendage resembling a live lobster became detached during combat; the [detached appendage](/Birdcage_Casefiles/items/FUNGUS_CRAB_Lobster_Appendage.html) retained the ability to fire lightning bolts when loaded with teeth as ammunition

## Status

Destroyed by radioactive detonation, July 2005. See [Albuquerque Green Box](/Birdcage_Casefiles/locations/Albuquerque_Green_Box.html) (item 25) for details on the weapon used.
