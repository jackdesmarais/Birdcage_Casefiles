# The Milkman

A mysterious figure whose presence has been noted throughout the history of [The Damn Vannes](/Birdcage_Casefiles/factions/The_Damn_Vannes.html). The mastermind of the Dairy Lobby.

## Aspects
- **Mastermind of the dairy lobby**
- **Daedalian plans and curving paths**
- **Deeply lactose-intolerant and allergic to almonds**

## Role in History

Sighted repeatedly as [The Damn Vannes](/Birdcage_Casefiles/factions/The_Damn_Vannes.html) rose to power under [Sean Claude](/Birdcage_Casefiles/NPCs/Sean_Claude.html). His true goal — revealed only after [Sean Claude's](/Birdcage_Casefiles/NPCs/Sean_Claude.html) assassination — was to ensure that [Pierce Sisnan](/Birdcage_Casefiles/NPCs/Pierce_Sisnan.html) and [Maddie Holmes](/Birdcage_Casefiles/NPCs/Maddie_Holmes.html) would find each other.
