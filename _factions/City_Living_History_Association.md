# City Living History Association

A Living History Association they put on exhibits about the history of the city. In order to inncrease "attendence" at their "events" they send agents into the past to "improve audience compatibility".

Agents are provided cover, resources, and activation codes as needed. Opposition agents from rival organizations can be identified for bonus "drink tickets" at CLHA social events.
