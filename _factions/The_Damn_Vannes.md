# The Damn Vannes

A paramilitary organization operating in and around The City. Originally led by [Sean Claude](/Birdcage_Casefiles/NPCs/Sean_Claude.html), now led by [Maddie Holmes](/Birdcage_Casefiles/NPCs/Maddie_Holmes.html) following his assassination.

Controls significant interests including the Mountain Pass rare earth metal mine (producing cerium, lanthanum, neodymium, and europium) in the Mojave desert.
