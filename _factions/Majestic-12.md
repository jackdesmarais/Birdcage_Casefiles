# Majestic-12

Also known as MJ-12. A clandestine U.S. Air Force unit with apparent knowledge of and operational interest in unnatural entities. Operated at cross-purposes to Delta Green in Albuquerque, New Mexico, July 2005. Their unit sustained heavy casualties and lost at least one recovered entity during [Operation Minimalism](/Birdcage_Casefiles/session_notes/DG_Casefiles/2026_05_31_Minimalism1.html).
