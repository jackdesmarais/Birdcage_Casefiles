# Kuoleman Enkelit

A meth and gun running biker gang out of Albuquerque.

Members exhibited unnatural invulnerability to lethal force. Some members were capable of performing rituals in proto-Uralic linked to the worship of Tuonetar, the Finnish goddess of death and the underworld. Their activities attracted the attention of Delta Green in July 2005.

Highly disrupted following [Operation Minimalism](/Birdcage_Casefiles/session_notes/DG_Casefiles/2026_05_31_Minimalism1.html), with casualties high.
