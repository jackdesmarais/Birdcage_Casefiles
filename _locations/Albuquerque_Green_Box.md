# Albuquerque Green Box

A Delta Green cache built from buried shipping containers, accessed via a padlocked horizontal hatch flush with the desert ground somewhere outside Albuquerque, New Mexico. Used as a fallback point during [Operation Minimalism](/Birdcage_Casefiles/session_notes/DG_Casefiles/2026_05_31_Minimalism1.html).

*Source: https://throneofsalt.blogspot.com/2023/04/another-green-box.html?m=1*

2. Pallet of 300 cans of _Chef Boyardee_ ravioli.

18. Fishtank filled with coffee grounds, kitchen refuse, and bulbous blue-white fungus. Paper taped to side: _"I eat plastic! Yum yum yum!"_ Half-consumed _He-Man_ action figure visible inside.

24. Nonstandard tarot deck; contains an uneven mix of Queen of Swords, Devil, Tower, 7 Cups and 4 Wands from different decks. Additionally contains "The Temple", "The Summit", "Page of Roots" and "6 of Threes".

Three fingers remain.**

27. Handheld Geiger counter. Plastic casing pried open and repaired with hot glue and duct tape. Laminated index cards attached to handle provide instructions on taking readings of atmospheric doroganon. No explanation of doroganon is provided, but it is repeatedly stated that levels above 4.5 are unfit for long-term human habitation.

31. Contract of sale for the immortal soul of a reactionary conservative podcaster. Purchaser's signature is smudged and faded, and cannot be read.
32. Conversational Diné Bizaad (Navajo) learning course on cassette.
33. Shoebox of goodbye letters, photographs of pets; assorted coins, small stones, pressed flowers, blackout poetry.
34. HAM radio set. Microphone cord has been cut. Actively transmitting a looping message: heavily-distorted male voice reads out names of each M, K and G class star within 150 light years of Earth, with each name followed by a sequence of SOS distress signals in assorted encodings.
35. Wooden saint's icon of an elephant, painted in classical Ethiopian Tewahedo Orthodox style.
36. Proto-Uralic to English dictionary.
